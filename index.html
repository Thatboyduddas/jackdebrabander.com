How it connects

Each business feeds the next one.

  1. Strategy Data Solutions

    Clean data in

    Lead lists are bought in bulk, then every number is checked for line type, Do Not Call status and litigator history before anyone sees it.

  2. Strategy B CRM

    Conversations, automatically

    Workflows text the clean list, answer replies in seconds and put booked calls on the calendar, with an AI assistant drafting the next message.

  3. Estes Health Solutions

    Families covered

    On the call, I compare private plans for the household and help them enroll. What we learn on those calls shapes the next list and the next workflow.

About Jack

I build the tools I wish I had, then sell them.

Jack DeBrabander is an entrepreneur and a licensed health insurance agent (NPN 21774171). He started in health insurance sales, running a team that texts and calls thousands of people a day.

The software on the market couldn't keep up, so he built Strategy B, the CRM his teams now run on and other agencies use. Bad phone lists were costing money and compliance risk, so he built Strategy Data Solutions to buy data and scrub it clean. Through Estes Health Solutions, he still works with families directly, comparing private health plans and helping them enroll.
